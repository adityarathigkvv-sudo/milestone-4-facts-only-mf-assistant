# Sample Q&A

These examples are aligned to the current prototype corpus. Every factual answer contains one official source link and a source-date line.

## 1. What is the exit load of SBI Large Cap Fund?
SBI Large Cap Fund has an exit load of 0.25% for exit within 30 days, 0.10% for exit after 30 days and within 90 days, and nil after 90 days from allotment.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/all-sbimf-schemes-factsheet-march-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=4659a7f0_4
Last updated from sources: 31 Mar 2026.

## 2. What is the benchmark of SBI Flexicap Fund?
The first-tier benchmark for SBI Flexicap Fund is BSE 500 (TRI).
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-flexicap-fund-factsheet-march-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=39a6ea88_2
Last updated from sources: 31 Mar 2026.

## 3. What is the lock-in period of SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a statutory lock-in period of 3 years.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/all-sbimf-schemes-factsheet-march-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=4659a7f0_4
Last updated from sources: 31 Mar 2026.

## 6. How do I download my capital-gains statement?
SBI Mutual Fund says existing investors can select “I am an Existing Investor” and then “Get Statements”; available statements include Account Statement, Capital Gains Statement and Smart Statement.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/ways-to-invest
Last updated from sources: Current page checked Sep 2026.
